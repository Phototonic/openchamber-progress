# A tidier rail and plan header

## Fixes

- **Rail: more room to read your plan.** **Copy update** and **Handoff note** now sit beside **Hide quiet** as small buttons and wrap below it when space is tight. They still open the same previews.
- **Plan header: linked work stays on one row.** Linked pull requests and issues no longer wrap onto a second row and push the progress bar down. The links that fit stay visible and the rest are under **N more**, or behind a single **N linked** button on a narrow rail.
- **Sub-agents: see what is happening now first.** The Sub-agents line leads with waiting, running, failed and interrupted counts. Finished and unknown counts move under **Show more**, and the line says **Nothing running now** when nothing is.
- **Board: reach the last plan without the counts note getting in the way.** In **Document** view on a wide board, the plan list and search results scroll in their own column. **Counts may be incomplete.** stays below the list, where you can still open its link. The page scrolls normally in narrow **Select** mode and in phone landscape.

## Misc

- Documentation: updated screenshots and directions to match the current board, rail and Setup, and removed unused images.
- Development: repaired on-demand browser checks for the current layout and updated the notes on how those checks find board controls. The worker-crash recovery test now explains what was refused if it fails.
- Development: updated the CodeQL code-scanning action.
