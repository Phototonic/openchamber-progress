# Faster with large libraries, and Tidy

## New

- **Full page: Tidy archives or deletes closed plans together.** The button beside Search includes finished, cancelled and failed plans matching your search and filters, or all closed plans in the current view when neither is set. Archive offers Undo; permanent deletion needs a separate confirmation. Counts and confirmations use the filters the board shows, including matching plans that haven't loaded into the list. If a refresh changes that scope, Tidy cancels an open confirmation. A count that may have missed plans says so.
- **Full page: select plans to archive or delete together.** Select individual plans or all matching your search and filters, including plans still in progress, with a warning before acting. In Archived plans, select plans to restore them.

## Improvements

- **Full page: large plan libraries stay responsive.** In a synthetic library of 511 plans, opening a plan fell from about 6 seconds to 0.2 seconds, and map dragging went from pauses of about a second to smooth frames around 17 milliseconds.
- Map: the desktop rail map grows with the plan, giving larger plans more room. Focus current work is back beside Fit, and shorter map help keeps the controls easy to find. Arrows from an in-progress prerequisite to another in-progress task can now show moving beads, and Focus current work announces the overview when there is nothing to focus.

## Fixes

- Plan list: the scrollbar stays draggable through background refreshes.
- Counts: zero no longer appears as "0+", and plus signs apply only to positive counts that may really be higher, including plan row badges after a failed refresh and Running and Waiting when a very long run list was cut short.
- Reporting: agents are now instructed to stop reporting to plans you archived or deleted and not recreate them unless you specifically ask. This is guidance, not automatic enforcement of an agent's behaviour. If work returns to the prerequisites of a task reported in progress, agents are told to mark that task blocked, or set it back to pending if none of its own work was done. A restored plan shows its earlier waiting delegations as retired, and a late report to an archived plan no longer prompts for document access.

## Misc

- Compatibility: archiving or deleting in-progress plans requires the 1.5.1 service. A 1.5.0 service cannot restore an archived in-progress plan; restore it before rolling back if you need it to keep receiving updates.
