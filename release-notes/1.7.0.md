# Find plans faster and open linked work

## New

- **Board: search and find what needs you from the top bar.** Search for plans or tasks, toggle **Hide quiet** beside **Filters**, or open **Needs you** for all plans and narrow it to this plan. When your quiet-plan choice only lasts while the view is open, the switch's tooltip says so.
- **Rail and board: switch views and find plan actions without digging through menus.** **Copy update** and **Handoff note** are in the plan header. Switch directly between **Map**, **List**, **Document** and **History**. The plan's More menu is just for **Sessions** and **Finish as cancelled** when available; use the **Settings** gear for Language, Setup and Troubleshooting.
- **Rail and board: open linked pull requests and issues from the plan header.** Links from the plan's main session appear as chips. Select one to open it through OpenChamber without looking it up in Sessions.
- Rail and board: the saved plan document uses the available height, with a one-line header and a short saved-age note. Wide tables scroll sideways on their own. When you switch between Map, List and Document on the same plan, you can pick up where you left off until you change plans or a newer capture arrives.

## Fixes

- **Setup: continue unfinished work after a reboot changes the store's device number.** Reopen Setup and choose **Continue** or **Resume** when offered. This works for operations that recorded proof of the original storage on directly mounted, single-device btrfs with Linux 6.7 or newer and a disk WWN (a hardware identifier). Setup still refuses snapshots and copies; older records without that proof and other storage keep their previous checks. (follow-up to the device-number issue reported by [@PhilippLoewen](https://github.com/PhilippLoewen))
- Setup: on systems that don't provide reliable file creation times, recovering an interrupted first save now reports success instead of **Waiting for another setup action**.
- Board: History's **Refresh** button stays in place when task details finish loading.

## Misc

- Development: added a native btrfs check that verifies Setup can continue on the original drive after its device number changes and refuses a snapshot or copy without writing to it.
- Development: made three release checks more reliable, covering paged sub-agent runs, the managed-host release journey and the browser check for prerequisite changes.
- Documentation: clarified that the companion keeps a limited amount of delivered note text in memory per session and does not save it to disk.
