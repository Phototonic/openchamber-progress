# Faster Windows startup and a Needs you inbox

## Fixes

- **Windows: the service starts quickly, and upgrading an old installation no longer depends on one long Setup request.** Setup moves the migration forward in short steps, shows progress and precise errors, and lets you pause and explicitly resume without losing history. Pausing after capture is disabled leaves it off until you resume. Fresh configuration still uses the older flow and can time out on machines with very slow PowerShell; the short-step fix covers migration, not every Setup operation.
- **Reporting: parallel sub-agents no longer lose a waiting assignment just because a sibling reported unrelated progress.** Changes affecting the assigned work still refuse the handoff, with an explanation rather than a silent rebase. Combined final reports also work from other worktrees.

## New

- **Progress: a Needs you inbox brings decision and review blockers together across active plans.** Open an entry to reach the task that needs attention; other reported blockers stay separate under Also blocked. The inbox reflects deliberate reports, not guesses about what you should do.
- **Languages: choose Simplified Chinese for the interface and progress summaries**, alongside English and Russian. Saved plan and task text stays as written. (thanks to [@kermis145](https://github.com/kermis145))

## Improvements

- **Phones: the rail and fullscreen board are available in OpenChamber on phones**, with responsive layouts, larger touch targets and a dependency map you can tap, pan and pinch. Physical-phone acceptance is scheduled for the live release; current layout and touch checks use browser emulation.
- **Delegation: waiting tasks say “Next sub-agent will take this”.** Refused handoffs explain why they could not bind. A waiting note is not an assignment or a task-status report.
- **Reporting: finish a plan and report its final task changes in one call.** The changes and finish outcome commit together, or neither does. A handoff without explicit task changes still does not complete tasks.
- Reporting: indexed handoff lookups keep combined final-report checks efficient as retained history grows.

## Misc

- Compatibility: still requires OpenChamber 2.7.1 or later. The reporting protocol and storage schema remain at version 2.
- Documentation: refreshed setup, reporting and compatibility guidance describes the supported paths and their limits.
